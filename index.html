CURATORIAL MANIFESTO
"We champion radical, genre-defying contemporary practices at the intersection of material sculpture, speculative digitalism, and raw visceral painting. No beige art. No polite compromises."

Founded in New York's Lower East Side, PRAXIS operates as a laboratory for uncompromising ideas. We reject decorative market complacency in favor of audacious physical presence, intense materiality, and conceptual courage.
